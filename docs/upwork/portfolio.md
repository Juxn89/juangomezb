## The form

| Field | Limit | What goes in |
|---|---|---|
| Project title | 70 characters (no counter shown) | English version of the Workana title |
| Your role | optional | "Solo developer: architecture, backend, AI and frontend" (the bot: "strategy, backend and dashboard") |
| Project description | 600 characters | A summary of the Workana description, with the measured figures |
| Skills and deliverables | 5, from Upwork's catalog | The Workana skills, by their Upwork names |
| Content | images (JPG/PNG/GIF, 10 MB), video (MP4 up to 100 MB, or YouTube/Vimeo), text, link, PDF, audio | Cover image, demo video, gallery, a Markdown text block with the full Workana description, and a link to the case study |

Each image and video takes a caption of up to 140 characters. The text block has a Plain text / Markdown toggle:
choose Markdown before pasting, or `**bold**` and bullets show literally. The link block validates the URL by
fetching it; juangomezb.com failed once (cold start) and passed on the retry, and its card shows only
"juangomezb.com", with no title or image. The thumbnail is chosen at the last step; the profile lists the newest
project first, and "Reorder portfolio" (the arrows next to +) fixes the order.

## Descriptions (600 characters)

**booking-agent.** Customers book by chatting as they would with a receptionist ("a cleaning Monday morning"). An AI
assistant finds the service, the professional and a free slot in the real calendar and holds it for five minutes;
the customer confirms with a button, and the AI cannot confirm or cancel anything. PostgreSQL makes double booking
impossible: 50 simultaneous requests, one booking. Email reminders and a live panel. Measured: 28/28 test
conversations as expected, USD 0.0024 per conversation.

**docs-assistant.** An AI assistant you add to your website with one line of code. It answers customers 24/7 from
your own documents (policies, catalog and FAQs as PDF, Word or web pages) and shows where every answer came from. It
doesn't make things up: when the answer isn't in your documents it says so and offers a person, and you get an
email with the whole conversation. Answers in English or Spanish. Measured on 54 test questions: 0 invented answers,
12/12 manipulation attempts blocked, USD 0.33 per 1,000 questions.

**invoice-extractor.** Upload photos or PDFs of invoices and receipts and get a spreadsheet ready for the books. The
AI reads each document but never has the last word: accounting rules check every number (lines add up to the
subtotal, subtotal plus tax gives the total, valid tax rate and tax ID). What passes is approved on its own; anything
doubtful goes to human review, with the original alongside and the failing field flagged. Excel or CSV export, no
duplicates. Measured: 0 of 42 false auto-approvals, USD 0.00065 per document.

**data-chat.** Upload your sales spreadsheet and ask the way you'd ask an analyst, in English or Spanish. You get the
figure, a chart, a one-line summary and the query that was used. The AI never writes SQL or touches your data: it
maps your question to the metrics you confirmed, and the server builds a read-only query. If a question is ambiguous
it asks, and every figure in the summary is checked against the result. Measured: 75/75 test questions right, 20/20
injection attempts with no effect, USD 0.00058 per question.

**aether-forge.** Automated trading bot for Capital.com, connected only through its API (REST and WebSocket), no
MetaTrader. It reads the market, decides the trade, sizes it to risk 1% of the account and places the stop and target
directly at the broker. Status: demo phase, running 24/7 on a server with a Capital.com demo account since October
20, 2026; it doesn't trade real money yet. Automatic cutoffs at a 5% daily, 10% weekly or 15% peak-to-trough loss.
Not financial advice, and no profit is promised.
